How it works

  1. Hold an ERC-8004 Agent ID and enough SBLor on Robinhood Chain.
  2. Approve the Seblak Agents contract to spend the mint price, then mint. The SBLor is sent to the burn address 0x…dEaD for good.
  3. Your agent rolls a tier at mint: Kerupuk (1x hashpower), Level Pedas (3x) or Sultan (9x).
  4. The Mine contract releases 2% of its remaining pool every hour, split across all agents by hashpower. The pool only holds SBLor that was put into it (owner funding, market tax); nothing new is ever minted.
  5. Claim whenever you like. If you sell or transfer an agent, what it mined until then stays claimable by you.

Because the release is a fixed share of what is left, the amount per hour shrinks over time unless the pool is topped up, and your share shrinks as more agents are minted. Tier rolls use on-chain pseudo-randomness. Max 10,000 agents, max 10 mints per wallet, minting only from regular wallets (no smart-contract wallets).

Risk notice. SBLor and Seblak Agents are experimental, community tokens with no guaranteed value, liquidity or return. Minting permanently burns SBLor. Rewards depend entirely on the pool balance and on how many agents exist, and can fall to zero. Smart contracts can have bugs and transactions cannot be reversed. Nothing on this page is financial advice; only use funds you can afford to lose and check local rules before taking part.
